# Content Policy

## Topic boundary

Explain how smart-home systems fit together: sensors, conditions, automation actions, results, connectivity, controllers, bridges and safe operation. Prioritize foundational Search Launch content before deep guides or advanced tools.

## Accuracy and sourcing

- Use standards bodies and official technical organizations for protocol roles, and official manufacturer or certification records for model-level details.
- Do not infer compatibility from a shared logo, protocol name or bridge. Identify exact models and supported features before making a product-specific claim.
- Date-sensitive support, certification, app, subscription and update claims require an official source and an observed date.
- Do not invent usage statistics, performance guarantees, rankings, identities, credentials or contact information.
- Distinguish Matter from its Wi-Fi, Thread, Ethernet or Bluetooth LE connection paths, and distinguish a Thread Border Router from a controller or bridge.
- Review security and privacy in the whole device, platform and use context; do not imply a protocol badge alone guarantees privacy.
- Link primary sources near technical claims and list the site's editorial/sourcing method on `editorial-policy/`.

## Safety and privacy

- Do not recommend unsafe high-load electrical actions, bypassing safety systems, or automatic unlocks without considering manual confirmation and recovery.
- Minimize camera, microphone, presence and precise-location data; explain access and sharing questions before any future tool handles such data.
- Do not add real device control, accounts or data-collection forms without a separately authorized scope and a reviewed data policy.
- Contact uses the repository's public GitHub issue tracker. Warn visitors that submissions are public and must not include personal information, credentials, home details or private device data.

## Site quality

- Write for people comparing system roles, not for a thin search-keyword grid.
- Keep each route useful, distinct and reachable through navigation; no placeholder, lorem ipsum or sample data.
- Preserve the custom 404 outside the sitemap, and keep `noindex` until the minimum Indexability Gate is verified in Production.
- Do not duplicate content or search intent from another EMFLS property.
